# Include Design Commentary

Why this syntax? Why `namespace`s? Why this specific feature?

C^4 was a idea I had when I was coding some C++, and though "I don't like this feature", "Nobody should use this", and things like that. All my ideas came from fixing other languages, or unique things I think languages are missing. That's how C^4's design came to be. A google doc. My include system was one of my first major motivators for this language. "Only 40 more features till the include system" "Only 20 left" "Only 3" and so on. I designed the include system over a 3 hour brainstorming session. This `namespace` based include system is why C^4 exists.
